# PulseNews AI — Product Vision

## 1. Executive Summary

PulseNews AI is an AI-powered news intelligence platform that transforms the daily firehose of global news into structured, exam-ready knowledge. It collects live updates from trusted RSS feeds, government press releases, and official YouTube channels, then uses AI to summarize, classify, rank by exam importance, and generate practice questions — all delivered through a modern short-video feed, live broadcast hub, and conversational AI tutor.

The platform serves competitive exam candidates (UPSC, SSC, banking, state services), professionals who need current affairs briefings, and researchers who require verified, source-tracked information.

---

## 2. Vision Statement

**To become the definitive AI layer between raw global news and exam-ready knowledge — where every breaking headline is automatically distilled into a study-ready fact within minutes of publication.**

---

## 3. Mission

1. **Collect** — Aggregate news from 30+ trusted sources including RSS feeds, government APIs (PIB, RBI, ISRO), and official YouTube channels in real time.
2. **Distill** — Use AI to extract the 3 most important facts from every article, classify by topic, and rank by exam relevance.
3. **Educate** — Transform distilled news into MCQs, flashcards, revision notes, daily PDF digests, and an interactive knowledge graph.
4. **Tutor** — Provide a conversational AI mentor that answers questions using RAG over the entire knowledge base.
5. **Verify** — Track source provenance and cross-reference facts across multiple outlets for verification scoring.

---

## 4. Problem Statement

### The Information Overload Crisis

Competitive exam candidates face an impossible daily challenge:

- **Volume**: Over 50,000 news articles are published daily across English-language sources alone.
- **Scattering**: Relevant information is spread across newspapers, TV channels, YouTube, government websites, and press releases.
- **Time**: Candidates spend 2-3 hours daily collecting, reading, and manually summarizing current affairs.
- **Quality**: Coaching institutes produce weekly compilations that are often 3-7 days behind real-time events.
- **Verification**: Social media is rife with misinformation; students struggle to distinguish verified facts from rumors.
- **Retention**: Reading news passively produces poor retention. Active recall (quizzes, flashcards) is proven to be 2x more effective but requires manual effort to create.

### Existing Solutions Fall Short

| Solution | Limitation |
|----------|-----------|
| Newspapers | No interactivity, no quizzes, no personalization |
| Coaching institute PDFs | 3-7 day delay, one-size-fits-all, no real-time updates |
| YouTube current affairs channels | Passive video, no structured notes, no search |
| News aggregator apps | No exam focus, no AI summaries, no quiz generation |
| AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini) | No real-time news, no source verification, hallucination risk |

## 7. Guiding Principles

1. **Real-time first** — News should appear within minutes, not days, of publication.
2. **Exam relevance over completeness** — Not every news item matters for exams. AI ranks importance 1-100.
3. **Active learning over passive reading** — Every news item ships with MCQs and quick-take bullets.
4. **Source transparency** — Every fact is traceable to its original source URL and publication timestamp.
5. **Mobile-first video** — The primary interface is a vertical short-video feed, not a text list.
6. **AI as assistant, human as gatekeeper** — AI generates content; a human QC gate can approve before publishing.
